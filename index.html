About Zatent

Empowering collaboration between students and entrepreneurs

Zatent is designed for idea-driven learning and entrepreneurial discovery. Students contribute creative solutions while entrepreneurs gain access to fresh talent and new perspectives.

For Students

Pitch ideas, work on real challenge briefs, learn from business owners, and grow your portfolio through teamwork and mentorship.

For Entrepreneurs

Share your task, receive student proposals, and collaborate with motivated innovators who want to bring your vision to life.

For Projects

Create a clear task, invite submissions, and evaluate student ideas to discover the best concepts and partnerships.

How it works

Simple steps to start collaborating

1. Post a Task

Entrepreneurs submit a challenge or brief with goals, timelines, and desired outcomes.

2. Submit Ideas

Students review tasks and send their ideas or proposals directly through the platform.

3. Collaborate

Teams and entrepreneurs communicate, refine concepts, and work together toward a practical solution.
